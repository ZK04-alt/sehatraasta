Medical records, kept together

SehatRaasta keeps medical documents and visit notes together on an Android phone. It works offline and offers English, Urdu and Pashto interface options.

Save papers under a patient, find older visits, and choose what to include in a PDF. You can also save a backup or transfer a selected visit to another device.

Where records stay

Records stay in the app on your phone. There is no automatic cloud upload or synchronisation. Exported PDFs, transfer files and backups are not encrypted by the app. Keep them protected and save a backup before uninstalling.

Install on Android

  1. Download the APK below on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser for this download.
  3. Tap Install, then open SehatRaasta. Turn off the browser installation permission afterwards.

Android 7.0 or later on an arm64-v8a or x86_64 device is required. Earlier testing copies use a different signing certificate. Save a full backup before uninstalling an older copy, then restore it in this release. Keep that backup until you have checked your restored records. PDFs are not full backups.

Medical advice

SehatRaasta organises records. It does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment. Ask a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
