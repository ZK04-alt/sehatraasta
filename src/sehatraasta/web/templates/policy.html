Local data

SehatRaasta stores patient records, referral bundles, reported costs, documents and activity records on the device running the app. Records are not sent to a cloud service.

Browser storage

A session cookie holds interface language and form protection information. It does not contain patient records and is not a login. Pages are marked no-store.

Access

The app has no user accounts, access roles or application-level encryption. Protect access to your device. Backups and exports are not encrypted.

Files and deletion

Remove hides a paper, visit or patient from active records. Its records and original files remain on this device in Removed items and are included in later whole-dataset backups. Restore returns them to active records. Permanent deletion is a separate confirmed action in Removed items; failed file cleanup remains pending and can be retried. This is not a guarantee of forensic erasure. Separate originals, exports, backups, printouts and restored copies must be removed separately. There is no automatic retention schedule.

No analytics

The app has no analytics, advertising or background telemetry.

Camera and selected files

The camera is optional and used only when Scan QR is selected. Camera images are processed on the device, not uploaded or kept as patient records. The file picker grants access only to the documents selected for import, storage or backup. Saving a PDF or archive to a cloud-backed folder can send it to that folder's provider; SehatRaasta does not control that provider.

Support

Report software issues at github.com/ZK04-alt/sehatraasta/issues. Do not include personal information or private documents.

Using SehatRaasta

SehatRaasta organizes referral information and supporting documents. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. It does not recommend treatment, interpret clinical results or determine medical urgency. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. Contact local emergency services for urgent medical help.

Documents

Only add documents you are permitted to store. Text is shown as entered. File checks do not establish clinical accuracy or confirm that a file is harmless. Verify source documents and review status.

Records and backups

Check saved records and keep verified backups. Device loss, interrupted operations or storage failures can affect access to records.

Language

Interface language selection does not translate source documents or verify their accuracy.
