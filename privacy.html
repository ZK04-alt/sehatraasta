This describes the SehatRaasta 1.6.0 evaluation build. Independent privacy/security review is pending. Use fictional records while testing.

Information stored

SehatRaasta stores the patient details, visits, reported history, medicines, test requests and results, imaging, instructions, expenses, documents and local activity records entered or selected by the user. Unfinished entries can also be saved locally. Only add another person's records with permission.

Where processing happens

In the Android app, records and imported documents are held in app-private storage on the phone. The app has no developer-controlled cloud service, user accounts, advertising or analytics. It does not automatically send records to the developer. The desktop website stores records on the computer running it; phone and computer records are not automatically synchronised.

The Android app uses an authenticated local loopback connection to its own on-device service. That service is not an internet patient-record server. External websites cannot be opened in the embedded app interface.

Camera and selected files

Camera access is optional, requested for QR scanning. Camera frames are decoded locally and are not uploaded or saved as medical documents. Alternatively, the user can select a saved QR image. A QR contains a random lookup reference, not the medical record. It works where the matching visit is already saved or imported.

The system file picker gives access to files the user selects. The app does not request unrestricted access to the device's files. Imported copies are stored in app-private storage with generated names; the original files in other folders remain separate.

Sharing and recovery

The user chooses whether to export a PDF, a one-visit passport archive or a whole-dataset backup, and where to save it. These exports can contain sensitive information. They are not encrypted by SehatRaasta. Saving to a cloud-backed folder or sharing through another application can send information to that provider under its own policies. SehatRaasta cannot remove those independent copies.

Access and security limits

There is no app-level login, access-role system or additional database encryption. Protect the phone with a device screen lock and keep recovery copies in a controlled place. The app's private storage and local connection are safeguards, not a promise of absolute security or an independent clinical/security certification.

Retention and deletion

Remove hides a paper, visit or patient from active records. The records and original files remain locally in Removed items, can be restored, and are included in later whole-dataset backups. Permanent deletion is a separate confirmed action in Removed items. Failed file cleanup remains pending and can be retried. This does not guarantee forensic erasure. Unfinished entries have their own removal controls. Clearing app data or uninstalling affects app-private copies; separate original files, exported PDFs, passport archives, backups, printouts and earlier restored datasets must be deleted separately. There is no automatic retention period or server-held account.

Logs and support

Local diagnostic and activity records are intended to record technical actions and references without copying medical text. Do not send patient details, medical files, screenshots containing health information or backups to a public issue tracker. Support correspondence, if sent, will be handled through the confirmed contact's provider; the publisher must document that process before publication.

Website, store and support

This project website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website visits under its privacy policy. Uptodown handles its store and download activity under its own privacy policy. These are separate from the app's local medical records.

Support emails are received through Gmail. The publisher uses the message and sender details to answer the request. Do not send patient records, backups or screenshots containing medical information. Ask through the support address if you want your support correspondence removed, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

Changes

The publisher will update this page if app data handling or distribution changes. This is a description of the app and its current limits, not a claim of legal or clinical certification.
